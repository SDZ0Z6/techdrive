KYC & AML

Built into the platform

Not a bolt-on module. Verification state drives wallet limits, withdrawal eligibility and risk scoring across the whole platform.

  • Identity verification — Document capture and verification at configurable trigger points
  • Risk-based tiering — Verification depth scaled to player risk profile and transaction volume
  • Sanctions and PEP screening — Integrated screening at onboarding and on an ongoing basis
  • Transaction monitoring — Automated detection of structuring, rapid cycling and other patterns, with an alert queue
  • Source of funds — Workflow for enhanced due diligence above configurable thresholds
  • Audit trail — Complete, immutable record of verification decisions and compliance actions
  • Reporting — Exports formatted for regulatory submission

Responsible gaming

Player protection tools

Every brand on the platform has these tools available. Under our licence, the mandatory subset is enabled by default and cannot be switched off.

Read our responsible gaming position

  • Deposit, loss and wager limits, set by the player
  • Session time limits and reality-check reminders
  • Cooling-off periods
  • Self-exclusion, temporary and permanent
  • Account closure on request
  • Age verification at registration
  • Links to support organisations, displayed in the player's language

Data protection

How player data is handled

  • Encryption in transit and at rest
  • Role-based access control with full audit logging
  • Data retention policies aligned to licence requirements
  • Encrypted backups held in geographically separate locations
  • Breach notification procedure with defined timelines

Client obligations

What sits with you

This section matters. Read it before signing anything with us or with anyone else.

Market authorisation

You are responsible for confirming that you are permitted to offer your services to players in each market you target, and for holding every licence, permit or authorisation those markets require. Our licence does not extend to markets it does not cover.

Legal advice

We are a technology provider. Nothing on this site or from our team constitutes legal advice. Take your own advice from qualified counsel in each market you intend to operate in.

Marketing conduct

Your acquisition activity must comply with the advertising rules of the markets you operate in, including restrictions on targeting minors and on promotional claims.

Player conduct

Where you handle player support directly, complaints, disputes and responsible gaming interventions are your responsibility, using the tools we provide.

Geo-restriction

You must apply and maintain blocking for any jurisdiction you are not authorised to serve. We provide the geo-blocking capability; configuring it correctly is your obligation.

If any of the above is unclear for your situation, raise it with us before you sign. We would rather turn away business than deliver a platform into a position that won't hold.
