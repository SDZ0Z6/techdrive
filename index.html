FAQ

Common questions

Anything not covered here, ask us directly — Telegram is usually fastest.

Do I need my own gaming licence?

Not for White Label — you operate under ours. Turnkey requires you to hold your own. If you're unsure which applies to you, ask us on a call.

How long does launch take?

White Label is typically live in weeks. Turnkey runs one to three months depending on customisation and licence timing. API integration is usually days.

How is it priced?

White Label and API are revenue share. Turnkey is a setup fee plus a monthly or revenue-share component. Exact terms depend on scope and volume.

Can I use my own payment providers?

Yes. We integrate additional channels on request alongside the ones already in the stack.

Who owns the player data?

Under Turnkey, you do — entirely. Under White Label, data is held on the platform and made available to you in full through the back office and reporting exports.

What support do I get after launch?

24/7 technical support, NOC monitoring, a named account manager, and platform updates at no additional cost.
