Due diligence

Four things we'd want you to check on any provider

Regional capability

Built for Asia-Pacific

Operating in this region is not a translation exercise. Payment behaviour, preferred verticals, device profiles and network conditions all differ from Western markets, and a product that ignores that converts badly.

  • Languages — English and Chinese at launch, with Thai, Bahasa Indonesia and Vietnamese available
  • Currencies — Regional fiat and crypto-denominated balances
  • Payment rails — Local bank and instant transfer networks, regional e-wallets, and stablecoin settlement
  • Content mix — Vertical weighting tuned to regional preference, including the fishing and lottery formats Western platforms tend to under-serve
  • Network engineering — Cross-border routing designed for the actual conditions in the region, not assumed connectivity
  • Support hours — 24/7 coverage aligned to regional peak play times
