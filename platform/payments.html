Routing and automation

  • Intelligent routing based on live success rate, cost and limits
  • Automatic failover when a channel degrades
  • Automated deposit crediting and withdrawal processing within configured thresholds
  • Manual review queue for transactions above threshold
  • Automated reconciliation and settlement reporting

Risk

  • Velocity and pattern checks on deposit and withdrawal behaviour
  • Configurable limits per player, tier and channel
  • Integrated with the platform risk engine and KYC status
