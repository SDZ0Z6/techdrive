Attack mitigation

Always on. There is no manual activation step and no window where traffic is unprotected while a scrubbing service spins up.

  • [[MITIGATION_CAPACITY]] volumetric DDoS protection — Always-on scrubbing, no manual activation delay
  • CC and application-layer protection — Behavioural detection of slow, low-volume attacks that volumetric filtering misses
  • Web Application Firewall — Managed rule sets with gaming-specific tuning
  • Rate limiting and bot management — Protecting login, registration and payment endpoints

Delivery and routing

Public routing between regions in Asia-Pacific is slow and unpredictable. Treating that as a solved problem is how platforms end up with a two-second lobby in their largest market.

  • Global CDN — Static assets and media served from edge nodes near your players
  • Cross-border route optimisation — Dedicated acceleration for traffic crossing regional network boundaries, where public routing is slow and unstable
  • Multi-line ingress — Multiple upstream paths so a single carrier problem doesn't take you offline

Availability

  • Multi-region deployment — Active infrastructure in more than one region
  • Automatic failover — Health-checked switchover without manual intervention
  • Domain and DNS management — Managed resolution with failover routing
