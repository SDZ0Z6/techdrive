Integration modes

Seamless or transfer — your call

Seamless Wallet

Players keep a single balance on your platform. Every bet and payout calls your wallet endpoint in real time. Nothing to transfer, nothing to reconcile between wallets, and the player never sees a boundary between games.

Best for: platforms that want one balance and the smoothest player experience.

Transfer Wallet

Funds move between your main wallet and per-provider wallets on demand. Lower coupling to your systems and easier to introduce if your wallet can't sustain real-time call volume.

Best for: platforms with existing wallet constraints or staged rollouts.

API questions

Can I choose which providers to enable?

Yes. Enable, disable and reweight providers per brand and per market from configuration.

Do you support my currency?

Multi-currency is supported including crypto-denominated balances. Confirm your specific requirement with us.

What happens if a provider goes down?

Health checks detect the failure and the affected provider is removed from the lobby automatically, so players never hit a dead game tile.
